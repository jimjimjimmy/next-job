# Your Story — Interview Narrative

## The 2-Minute Version (Elevator Pitch)

"I'm [Your Name], a [your title/level] engineer with [X years] of experience building [what you build]. My background is in [background], and I'm particularly interested in [area of expertise]. I've spent the last [timeframe] at [company] where I [key accomplishment]. I'm looking for my next role where I can [what excites you] and contribute to [type of impact]."

---

## The 5-Minute Version (Phone Screen / Coffee Chat)

### Background (1 minute)
[Where are you from? How did you get into engineering? Any early influences?]

Example: "I grew up in [location] and got interested in programming in [year/situation]. I studied [degree] at [university] and knew pretty quickly that I wanted to work as a software engineer."

### Career Journey (2 minutes)
[Walk through your jobs in order, highlighting growth and learnings]

Example: 
- "I started at [Company 1] as a [junior role], where I learned [key skill] and built [project]."
- "That led to [Company 2], where I took on more responsibility, leading [project] and growing from [role A] to [role B]."
- "Most recently at [Company 3], I've been [current role], where I've focused on [key work] and achieved [key result]."

### What You're Looking For (1 minute)
[What excites you now? What's next?]

Example: "I'm looking for a role where I can [specific growth area], ideally at a company focused on [mission/product], ideally working with [tech stack/team type]."

### Why This Company (1 minute)
[What specifically attracted you to them? What do you know about them?]

Example: "I'm excited about [Company's product/mission] because [specific reason]. I noticed you're using [tech stack] and building [what they're building], which aligns perfectly with my interests in [area]."

## Deep Dive Answers

### "Tell Me About Yourself"
[Your 2-minute version above]

### "What Are Your Strengths?"
Pick 2-3 and back them with examples:
- **Strength 1**: [Example from work showing this strength]
- **Strength 2**: [Example demonstrating this]
- **Strength 3**: [Example showing impact]

### "What Are Your Weaknesses?"
[Pick something real, show how you're working on it]

Example: "Early in my career, I sometimes moved fast without getting enough feedback from the team. I learned that the best solutions come from collaboration. Now I make sure to get input early and often, especially on design decisions."

### "Why Do You Want to Leave Your Current Role?"
[Be honest but professional]

Examples:
- "I'm looking for [new opportunity] that I don't have at my current place"
- "I want to work on [specific area] at a [stage/size] company"
- "I'm excited about [this company's mission] specifically"

### "Why Do You Want to Join [This Company]?"
[Be specific — mention their product, tech, mission, team]

### "Describe a Time You Failed"
[Pick a real example, show what you learned]

Format: Situation → Action → Result & Learning

### "Describe a Time You Overcame a Challenge"
[Similar format with positive outcome]

### "How Do You Approach [Technical Problem]?"
[Prepare examples from your work]

## Questions to Ask Interviewers

- "What does success look like in this role?"
- "What are the biggest challenges your team is facing right now?"
- "What's the tech stack and why did you choose it?"
- "How do you approach code review and knowledge sharing?"
- "What's your team structure and how does this role fit in?"
- "What does career growth look like here?"
- "What's the company culture like and how does engineering fit in?"
