## Bottom Line Positioning

Don't just say: "[I have relevant skills]"

Say: **"[Specific value] is what matters to [Company] right now, and here's why I'm the person to deliver it."**

Example:
> "The challenge isn't just AI features—it's making complex, AI-assisted systems understandable and trustworthy. That's what I do best."

## Research Checklist

- [ ] Read company blog / press releases
- [ ] Understand the founders' background
- [ ] Look up the interviewer on LinkedIn / Twitter / GitHub
- [ ] Try the product deeply (not just surface-level)
- [ ] Read recent news (funding, launches, partnerships, layoffs)
- [ ] Find the job description again
- [ ] Identify 3-5 competitors and understand differentiation
- [ ] Watch any public talks / podcasts from leadership
- [ ] Identify the company's biggest challenge (not stated, but implied)
