## Company Overview

**What They Do**  
StudyFetch is an AI-powered learning platform that transforms course materials (lectures, PDFs, PowerPoints, class notes, videos) into interactive study tools including flashcards, quizzes, practice tests, and AI-powered tutoring.

**Key Product Features**
- **Spark.e AI Tutor**: Grades essays, quizzes, tracks progress, provides tailored explanations
- **Interactive Study Guides**: Auto-generated from course materials
- **Flashcards & Practice Tests**: AI-created from notes and lectures
- **Progress Tracking**: Adaptive learning that personalizes to each student

**Impact**
- Used by **7.5+ million students worldwide**
- Recognized by White House, NVIDIA, Google as a leader in ethical AI education
- #1 AI-native learning platform for education

## Key Questions to Ask Phillip

### Product & Strategy
1. "What's your product vision for the next 12-24 months? What are the biggest product bets?"
2. "How do you think about the teacher vs. student experience? Which is your primary focus right now?"
3. "What's been your biggest learning since launch about how students actually use the product?"

5. "What role does design play in your product strategy? Can you tell me about a recent design decision that moved the needle?"

## Interview Strategy

**Positioning**
- Show **product thinking**: Ask about strategy, user behavior, metrics
- Demonstrate **learning**: Ask thoughtful questions that show you've researched
- Build **rapport**: Phillip is a fellow product person—connect on product philosophy
- Highlight **impact**: Talk about how your design affected business metrics

**Tone**
- Collaborative (not ego-driven designer vs. product)
- Curious about their space and challenges
- Confident in your ability to solve ambiguous problems
- Authentic about what excites you

**Red Flags to Avoid**
- ❌ Don't just ask about "design tools" or "design process"—he cares about impact
- ❌ Don't criticize current product (even if it has rough edges)
- ❌ Don't focus only on aesthetics—product designers own strategy
- ❌ Don't forget to ask about challenges (shows you're thinking practically)
