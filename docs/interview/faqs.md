## Questions for Interviewers

Keep this handy and pick 2-3 for each interview:

- "What does success look like in this role?"
- "What are the biggest technical challenges your team is facing?"
- "How does your engineering team approach code review?"
- "What's the tech debt situation? How do you prioritize paying it down?"
- "How do you measure engineering productivity?"
- "Tell me about a recent project you're proud of"
- "How is remote work handled?"
- "What does career growth look like for this role?"
- "What surprised you most about working here?"
